Vision

Why I work this way

At the core of everything I do is a desire to learn, grow, and make a positive impact. Whether it is solving a complex data problem or capturing a fascinating view with my camera, I am constantly looking for new challenges and better ways to understand the world.

In the future, I hope to keep blending technical work with photography, storytelling, and other creative instincts that make technology feel more grounded and meaningful.

Technology

How the interest began

I have been drawn to technology since I was young. Video games gave me an early path into code, from modifying online game environments to experimenting with a language derived from C.

That first exposure gradually grew into a broader interest in using computers to solve difficult problems. During my undergraduate training in biomedical sciences, I became increasingly interested in using data to uncover patterns in scientific and technical questions.

Photography

Where the camera became personal

My relationship with photography began during a trip to Syria, my family's homeland. In 2011, while my grandfather was seriously ill, we traveled to see him and spent time at his farm in the hills east of the city.

Using an early iPhone, I started taking photographs so I would not lose those memories. One image of cherries on a branch still matters deeply to me because it carries both the place and the person with it.

Languages

The part of me that keeps listening

Growing up in a bilingual Spanish-Arabic speaking family gave me an early love for language. I later learned English through a mix of challenge, internet culture, and online friendships, and by the time my family moved to the United States I was already fluent.

Since then I have continued learning for the pleasure of it. Portuguese, French, and Turkish each came into my life differently, and that process of learning how people speak and think has shaped the way I communicate just as much as any technical training.
