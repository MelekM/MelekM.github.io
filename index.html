Professional journey

Professional Journey

My journey into data science began with a passion for technology and problem-solving, which led me to earn a Master's degree in Data Science and AI from Northwestern University. I specialize in using data to generate insights and develop solutions, and I’ve worked on projects ranging from algorithmic optimization to predictive modeling.

During my studies, I developed a strong interest in algorithmic problem-solving and applied that interest to projects in this portfolio. Working across research, modeling, and visualization sharpened my ability to move from raw information to practical decisions.

My expertise extends to big data analysis, creating models, and using data-driven approaches to tackle complex issues. I enjoy combining rigor with clarity so the final result is useful, legible, and grounded in the problem it was meant to solve.
