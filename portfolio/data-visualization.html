Project Goals

  • Explore different visualization strategies for different data structures.
  • Practice visuals that support comparison, hierarchy, and change over time.
  • Connect analysis with storytelling so final outputs communicate more than raw numbers.

Areas of Focus

Temporal data: showing how values change over time without flattening the story.

Categorical data: comparing groups clearly while reducing label clutter.

Hierarchical data: preserving relationships across nested structures.

Why It Matters

This project group complements the modeling-heavy work in the portfolio. It shows the communication layer: the ability to choose the right visual form, organize evidence, and guide an audience through a conclusion.

What It Shows

  • Judgment around chart selection and visual hierarchy.
  • Comfort moving between exploratory notebooks and presentation-ready outputs.
  • Attention to narrative structure, not only technical correctness.
